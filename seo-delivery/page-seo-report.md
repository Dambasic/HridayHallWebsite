## 1. On-Page SEO Architecture & Overview

This document provides a page-by-page audit and optimization log across all public landing pages of **Hriday Banquet Hall**. Every page has been systematically upgraded with:
1. **Dynamic Page Titles**: Front-loaded with commercial intent keywords and constrained to under 60 characters to prevent SERP truncation.
2. **Unique Meta Descriptions**: Compelling copy with active CTAs under 155 characters.
3. **Strict Heading Hierarchy**: Exactly one semantic `<h1>` tag per route, supported by contextual `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags.
4. **Self-Referential Canonicalization**: Eliminating duplicate content and tracking parameter dilution.
5. **Full Social Graph**: Open Graph and Twitter Card tags with 1200x630 pixel previews.
6. **Descriptive Image Alt Attributes**: Replacing generic filenames with localized, keyword-rich descriptions.
